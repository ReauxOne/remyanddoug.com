01 · How it works

Two friends.
That's the whole app.

Remy remembers

Remy

The moment a thought shows up, tell Remy. Say it or type it. It gets filed for you: work or home, quick or big, where and when.

Hey Remy, remember washer fluid.
Doug does

Doug

Ask Doug what's next and say how much energy you have. You get one thing that fits your time, your place and your tank. Not a list. One thing.

Doug, what now? I'm running low today.
02 · Gentle by design

It does the remembering.
You do the living.

  • a
    One answer, never a list.You'll never open the app to forty-seven things.
  • b
    "Not now" is free.No red badges, no streaks, no guilt. Remy quietly remembers.
  • c
    Work stays at work.Set your hours. Outside them, work stays out of sight.
  • d
    Matches your energy.Low, some or full tank. Doug's suggestion fits how you feel.
